## Executive Summary

The feature correctly frames issue #246 as a **styles-owned open shell contract** (wrapper + overlay + dialog) that React applies when `open`, preserving interactive behavior in React only. That aligns with constitution principles **I** (styles owns visual/class contracts) and **IV** (React preserves parity by consuming styles, not inventing visuals). Research and the open-presentation contract are strong on root cause (`@extend` / missing shell vs bare portal), content-order (`column-reverse` → PathAble `column`), and Storybook/visual regression.

The design is ready for tasking **after locking a few contract ambiguities**: the **exact open visibility class list** (especially PathAble-only vs dual USWDS classes), and whether styles Storybook fixtures must prove PathAble-only CSS works without relying on `.usa-modal-wrapper`. Until those are explicit, FR-001/SC-002 “matching external open presentation” can silently diverge between packages. Backdrop click-to-dismiss is unspecified relative to USWDS norms and should be decided as a product question.
