Your first month in the employment programme

This guide explains what happens after you enrol, who you will meet and what to bring to each appointment.

Meeting your case worker

Within five working days of enrolling, your case worker will call to book an intake appointment. The appointment takes about an hour. You will talk about your work history, the support you need and the goals you want in your employment plan.

What to bring

  1. Photo identification
  2. Your most recent support plan
  3. Any letters from your employer about adjustments

If you cannot attend

Call your case worker at least one day before the appointment so it can be moved. You can also read about how to reschedule an appointment.
