Employment programme handbook

A handbook for participants, case workers and employers taking part in the programme.

Starting the programme

Everyone begins with an intake appointment and an agreed employment plan.

Your intake appointment

The appointment takes about an hour and covers your goals and your support needs.

Preparing for the appointment

Gather your identification and any letters about adjustments.

Documents to bring

Photo identification and your most recent support plan.

Accepted identification

A driver's licence, passport or state identification card.
