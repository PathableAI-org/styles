1. Complete your intake

    Meet your case worker to talk about your goals and what support you need.

    • Bring photo identification.
    • Bring proof of your current address.
    • Tell us about any accommodations you use.

  2. Agree your employment plan

    Your case worker writes the plan with you, including the training and job search steps you will take and the dates for each one.

  3. Attend your first review

    We check in after four weeks to see how the plan is going.
