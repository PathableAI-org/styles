Add the `pathable-card-group`, `pathable-card__img`, `pathable-card--media-right`, `pathable-card__media--inset` and `pathable-card__{header,body,footer}--exdent` classes, the wrappers for their USWDS `usa-card` counterparts.

Unlike `usa-card-group`, a `pathable-card-group` has no side margins, and a card in it fills the group at every width unless it has its own width or column class. A button that is a direct child of an exdent part keeps its padding and lines up with the text. A lone button that is a direct child of a card footer loses its right margin. A card group that is a direct child of `pathable-prose` keeps its own list reset and card spacing, and the group ignores margin-top, margin-bottom and padding-left utilities while a card in it ignores max-width and margin-bottom utilities. A button that is a direct child of a card header, body or footer is never wider than the part and wraps its label; a word breaks inside only when it alone is wider than the button's content box. Utilities for side padding, left margin and max-width do not apply to a button that is a direct child of an exdent part, just as a margin-right utility does not apply to a lone button in a card footer. A flag or media-right card stacks like a card with media when the card is narrower than 30rem, even from a 40em viewport, so the 15rem media column never squeezes the text at a large text size.

From a 40em viewport, a flag card whose width comes from its content (for example `width-auto` or a shrink-to-fit parent) is 30rem wide, or the viewport width if that is less, because its two-column layout depends on its own width. Below 40em a flag card is sized like any other card. From a 40em viewport, a flag card is an unnamed inline-size query container, so container queries and `cqi` or `cqw` units inside it resolve against the card.
