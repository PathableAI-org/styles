A `.pathable-fieldset` inside a `.pathable-form` now has a 24 px top margin, the same as a `.pathable-label`, so its legend is no longer flush against the field above it. A fieldset that is the first child of a `.pathable-form-group` keeps no margin of its own, so the group's spacing is not doubled. No class or token changed.
