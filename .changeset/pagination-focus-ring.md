Pagination page buttons, arrow links and the current page button now show the PathAble focus ring (Bright Blue Brooks) instead of the browser default. To keep the ring on its white background, the pagination has 4 px of padding above and below (8 px below at 640 px and narrower, or on a coarse pointer), with its margin reduced by the same amount. On a coloured surface its white box is therefore taller, and an element whose margin collapses with the pagination's sits up to 4 px (8 px) lower.
