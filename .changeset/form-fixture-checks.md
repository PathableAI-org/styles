Adds the `pathable-hint--required`, `pathable-form-group--error`,
`pathable-label--error` and `pathable-input--error` classes.

- The required asterisk is drawn in the theme's dark error colour.
- A single `pathable-select` with `pathable-input--error` keeps its usual
  height, so it lines up with error text inputs beside it.
