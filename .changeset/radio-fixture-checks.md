Adds the `pathable-radio__input--tile` class. On the input of a choice it draws
the label as a bordered tile with a larger tap target, and the checked, disabled
and `aria-disabled` states apply to the tile.

- The focus ring of a focused checked tile is PathAble ink, so it has at least
  3:1 against the tile tint.
