Add the `pathable-icon-list__title` and `pathable-icon-list--size-lg` classes, wrapping the matching USWDS `usa-icon-list` classes. The title is set at regular weight (400), and the large size removes the list's 72ex maximum width. A `pathable-icon` inside `pathable-icon-list__icon` is now sized 1.5rem square (2.25rem with `--size-lg`) instead of 1em. Long words in the content wrap instead of running out of it under large text on a narrow screen.
