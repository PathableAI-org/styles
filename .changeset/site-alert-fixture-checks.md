Adds `pathable-site-alert--no-icon`, wrapping `usa-site-alert--no-icon`, which hides the status icon and removes the text indent. Adds `pathable-site-alert--no-heading`, which sets the bold lead-in of a site alert with no heading in the PathAble bold weight and leaves its icon, padding and indent as they are with a heading.

The site alert's nested `pathable-alert` now gets the info or emergency surface, the status icon, the site-margin content width and the slim styles of a site alert. Links in an emergency site alert take the alert's white text colour, and its focus ring takes the danger text colour.
