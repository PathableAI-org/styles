Adds the `pathable-site-alert--no-icon` and `pathable-site-alert--no-heading`
modifiers.

- `pathable-site-alert--no-icon` hides the status icon and removes the text
  indent.
- `pathable-site-alert--no-heading` sets the bold lead-in of a site alert with
  no heading in the PathAble bold weight, and leaves its icon, padding and
  indent as they are with a heading.
