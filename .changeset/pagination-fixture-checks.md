Adds the `pathable-pagination__button`, `pathable-pagination__previous-page`,
`pathable-pagination__next-page`, `pathable-pagination__page-no` and
`pathable-pagination__link-text` classes.

- `pathable-pagination__page-no` takes the item style.
- `pathable-pagination__link-text` keeps the "Previous" or "Next" label on one
  line.
- `pathable-current` on a page button gets the USWDS current-page look.
