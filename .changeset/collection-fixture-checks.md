Add the `pathable-collection--condensed`, `pathable-collection__description`, `pathable-collection__meta-item`, `pathable-collection__calendar-date`, `pathable-collection__calendar-date-month` and `pathable-collection__calendar-date-day` classes, wrapping the matching USWDS `usa-collection` classes. A meta item that also has the `pathable-tag` class is drawn as a PathAble tag with the meta-item top margin, and a collection inside `pathable-prose` keeps its item, condensed and meta-item spacing. Long words in an item, tags included, wrap inside the body instead of running out of it under large text on a narrow screen.
