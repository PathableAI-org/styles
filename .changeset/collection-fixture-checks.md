Adds the `pathable-collection--condensed`, `pathable-collection__description`,
`pathable-collection__meta-item`, `pathable-collection__calendar-date`,
`pathable-collection__calendar-date-month` and
`pathable-collection__calendar-date-day` classes.

- A meta item that also has the `pathable-tag` class is drawn as a PathAble tag
  with the meta-item top margin.
- A collection inside `pathable-prose` keeps its item, condensed and meta-item
  spacing.
