Adds the `pathable-checkbox__input--tile` class. On the input of a choice it
draws the label as a bordered tile with a larger tap target, and the checked,
indeterminate, disabled and `aria-disabled` states apply to the tile.

- The focus ring of a focused checked or indeterminate tile is PathAble ink, so
  it has at least 3:1 against the tile tint.
