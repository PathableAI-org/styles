Add the `pathable-checkbox__input--tile` class, the wrapper for the USWDS `usa-checkbox__input--tile` modifier. On the input of a choice it draws the label as a bordered tile with a larger tap target, and the checked, indeterminate, disabled and `aria-disabled` tile states apply to it as they do to the USWDS class. There is no other visible change.
