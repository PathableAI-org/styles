Adds the `pathable-list--unstyled` modifier, wrapping USWDS `usa-list--unstyled`: no bullets or numbers, no left padding, no top or bottom margin, and items with no bottom margin and no width limit. It works the same inside `pathable-prose`. Markup that already used the class, such as the link groups in the big `pathable-footer`, now renders without bullets or indent, as the footer page intends.
