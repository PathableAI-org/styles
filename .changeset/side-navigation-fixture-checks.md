`pathable-sidenav__item` is now a class with a selector: it gives every list
item, at every nesting level, the 1 px light grey divider above it. The
current-page bar now uses the link colour, is 4 px wide at the left edge of the
link at every viewport width and nesting level, and is no longer hidden on
current links in a nested list. Links in `pathable-sidenav` now show a 2 px
focus ring with no offset, and Highlight in forced-colors mode.
