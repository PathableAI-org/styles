Adds `bg-*` and `text-*` utilities for the USWDS grayscale and basic colour
names, under their own names and without a prefix: `bg-gray-5` to `bg-gray-90`,
and `bg-red`, `bg-orange`, `bg-gold`, `bg-yellow`, `bg-green`, `bg-mint`,
`bg-cyan`, `bg-blue`, `bg-indigo`, `bg-violet` and `bg-magenta`, with the
matching `text-*` classes.

- The state colours (`bg-error`, `bg-warning`, `bg-success`, `bg-info`,
  `bg-disabled`) are included.
- The numbered system colours (`bg-red-50v`, `bg-gray-cool-60`) still have no
  class.
- The compiled stylesheet grows by about 4KB.
